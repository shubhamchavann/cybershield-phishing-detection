🔐 DEFENSE GUIDELINES

The 4 Golden Rules of Cyber Safety

🚫

Never Share OTP or PIN

No bank manager, electricity officer, or police will ever ask for your OTP or UPI PIN.

🔍

Verify Domain Spelling

Look for lookalike domains like sbl.com or paytm-kyc.xyz before entering credentials.

⏳

Pause on Urgency

Threats of immediate power cuts or account blocks are almost always social engineering scams.

🛡️
