THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

One Click Can Make a Difference

Cybercriminals often create urgency, fear or excitement to make victims act without thinking. Always verify before clicking links, sharing information or making payments.

✓ Never share your OTP or UPI PIN

✓ Check the website address carefully

✓ Avoid unknown payment links

✓ Verify urgent requests independently

✓ Use strong and unique passwords
