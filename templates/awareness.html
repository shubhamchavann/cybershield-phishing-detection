🛡️ CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS

Stay Safe From Phishing

Learn how scammers try to steal your personal and financial information.

🎣

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals pretend to be trusted organizations to steal sensitive information.

🔗

Check Links

Check the domain carefully before clicking a link or entering your login information.

🔐

Protect OTP

Banks and legitimate services should never require you to share your OTP, CVV or UPI PIN.

⚠️

Beware of Urgency

Scammers often create urgency by saying your account will be blocked or an offer will expire.

💰

Job & Prize Scams

Be careful with unexpected job offers, prizes and requests for registration or processing fees.

🏦

Banking Safety

Never share your password, ATM PIN, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN with anyone.
