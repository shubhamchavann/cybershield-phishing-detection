COMMON ONLINE THREATS

Types of Phishing & Digital Fraud

Understanding common scam techniques can help you avoid becoming a victim.
🎣

Phishing

Attackers impersonate trusted organisations and try to convince users to click malicious links or provide sensitive information.

Warning Signs

  • Suspicious website links
  • Unexpected login requests
  • Urgent account warnings
🔐

OTP Fraud

A scammer may pretend to be a bank employee, customer-care representative or another trusted person and ask for an OTP.

Remember

  • Never share an OTP
  • Never share your CVV
  • Verify unexpected calls
💳

UPI Fraud

Fraudsters may send fake payment requests, misleading links or convince victims to enter their UPI PIN.

Remember

  • UPI PIN authorises transactions
  • Check payment requests carefully
  • Never reveal your UPI PIN
🪪

Fake KYC Scam

Scammers claim that your bank account, SIM card or wallet will be blocked unless KYC is updated immediately.

Warning Signs

  • Threat of immediate suspension
  • Unknown KYC links
  • Requests for banking credentials
▦

QR Code Scam

Fraudsters may use QR codes to redirect users to deceptive pages or trick them into authorising payments.

Stay Safe

  • Verify unknown QR codes
  • Read payment details carefully
  • Don't approve unexpected payments
💼

Fake Job Scam

Fraudsters advertise attractive jobs and then request registration, training or processing fees from applicants.

Warning Signs

  • Guaranteed high salary
  • Upfront registration fees
  • Unverified recruiters
🎁

Lottery & Prize Scam

Victims are told they have unexpectedly won money, gifts or rewards and must pay a fee or provide personal information to claim it.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected prize messages
  • Requests for processing fees
  • Requests for bank information
👤

Social Media Scam

Criminals can create fake profiles or impersonate people you know to request money or sensitive information.

Stay Safe

  • Verify unusual requests
  • Check account authenticity
  • Enable two-factor authentication

GOLDEN RULES

Stop. Check. Verify.

Never allow urgency or fear to make the decision for you. Independently verify unexpected requests before sharing information or transferring money.

✓ Never share OTP, CVV, password or UPI PIN

✓ Check the sender and website address

✓ Don't trust unexpected payment requests

✓ Avoid clicking unknown shortened links

✓ Use two-factor authentication where available

✓ Report suspicious activity quickly
