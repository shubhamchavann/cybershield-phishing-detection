About the Project

This multilingual digital literacy platform is an educational CEP (Community Engagement Project) initiative designed to make digital knowledge simpler and safer for everyone, especially beginners.

Project Objective

To empower non-technical citizens with basic computing skills, smart safety habits, and risk analysis tools in their native languages to defend against daily cyber threats.

Key Features

Interactive multi-language selector, core computing/internet courses, emergency scam guidelines, heuristic web URL risk checker, phishing message score detector, and a progress tracker.

Future Scope

Future iterations will expand to include voice-based screen readers, offline usage support, artificial-intelligence based scam voice analyzers, and support for additional Indian languages.

Multilingual Digital Literacy Content Development

Making digital learning accessible and cybersecurity safety understandable for everyone across different languages.
